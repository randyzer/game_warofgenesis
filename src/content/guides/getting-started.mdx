War of Genesis Wiki is being prepared as a decision-support reference for [WoG] War of Genesis: Idle Loot. Phase 0 establishes the project identity and publishing foundation; it does not publish unverified game mechanics, market prices, or redeem codes.

## What this wiki will help answer

- Where should I farm?
- Which equipment should I keep or sell?
- How valuable is an item after Steam Market fees?
- Which class or build choice better supports my goals?

## Phase 0 scope

This initial local build contains project identity and architecture checks only. Beginner guides, classes, farming guidance, builds, codes, patch notes, and market guidance remain candidates for later sourced work.

The first proposed tool is a Steam Market fee and net profit calculator. It is recorded for the MVP but is not enabled or implemented in Phase 0.

## Accuracy boundary

War of Genesis: Idle Loot is treated as a distinct product from War of Genesis: Idle Heroes. Codes or gameplay claims will not be copied between them, and unsourced codes will not be published.
