This War of Genesis: Idle Loot player count reference records 20,062 In-Game on Steam Community at 13:58:13 Asia/Taipei on October 10, 2026. It is a historical observation, not a live counter. The developer separately reported an Early Access peak of about 52,800 concurrent players in the October 7 release announcement.

<h2 id="snapshot">The October 10 Steam Community observation</h2>

The captured Steam Community page for App 4891320 showed 20,062 In-Game. The time of capture matters: a number taken from a changing community page should travel with its observation time rather than be labeled as the current count indefinitely.

The same page showed 66 in Group Chat. That describes a different activity and is not a number to add to In-Game. This reference preserves the two labels instead of combining them into a larger apparent player count.

<h2 id="peak">The official Early Access peak</h2>

The October 7 release announcement reported approximately 52,800 concurrent players during Early Access. It is a peak over an earlier period, not a count at the October 10 observation time.

The homepage research also contained a more precise third-party historical peak. This page uses the developer's rounded figure and identifies its origin, rather than mixing different providers' counts as if they were the same measurement.

<h2 id="interpretation">What these figures can and cannot show</h2>

A simultaneous-player count is not a daily active-user total, a lifetime player count, a sales total, or revenue. A momentary count and a historical maximum measure different things; comparing only those two values does not establish a growth or decline trend.

No repeated time series or representative player-discussion sample supports a market conclusion here. To see the displayed count at a later time, use the [Steam Community page](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4891320). The number above will remain labeled as the October 10 snapshot.
