This War of Genesis: Idle Loot overview covers Steam App 4891320, the free-to-play Windows desktop loot RPG from New Normal Soft Inc. It features a transparent desktop character, automatic combat, equipment growth, and eligible loot trading through the Steam Community Market. It is the Steam Idle Loot game, distinct from the older Idle Heroes mobile title.

<h2 id="identity-release">Game identity and release dates</h2>

The official Steam listing identifies New Normal Soft Inc. as both developer and publisher. Early Access began on September 10, 2026; the official release followed on October 7, 2026. The release announcement and store listing support those separate milestones.

The version covered here is [WoG] War of Genesis: Idle Loot. Similar series names are not evidence that a mobile download, gift code, or account system belongs to this Steam application. Use the Steam application ID when matching official information to the game.

<h2 id="gameplay-platform">Desktop combat and equipment growth</h2>

The verified storefront describes automatic battle in a transparent corner of a Windows desktop. Weapons, armor, and jewels drop as the character fights. Leaving the game running is the documented idle-play scenario; a timer for rewards after completely closing the client is not documented by this material.

The store lists Windows requirements and 3 GB of available storage space. That is a Windows requirement, not an Android or iOS installation size. The game is listed as Free To Play with In-App Purchases. For the paid currency introduced in the release update, see the [G-Coins reference](/guides/in-app-purchases/).

<h2 id="activities">Classes, activities, and trading</h2>

The October release material names Knight, Ranger, and Wizard. The store describes Alliance Raid, World Boss, and PvP Arena alongside the equipment loop. These features establish the advertised activities, not their exact current unlock levels or a tested ranking of the classes.

Eligible loot can be traded on the Steam Community Market, where the verified market page describes Steam Wallet funds. The existence of trading does not establish cash income, a complete tradable-item list, or a reliable farming return. See [market basics](/guides/market/) for the scope of the verified trading evidence.
