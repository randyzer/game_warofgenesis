War of Genesis: Idle Loot in-app purchases exist alongside its Free To Play Steam listing. The October 7 update introduced G-Coins for paid products, purchasable from the Shop page. An older official FAQ also identifies disabled Steam Overlay as a possible cause of a specific Steam Wallet payment problem.

<h2 id="free-and-paid">Free play and paid products</h2>

The Steam store lists the game as Free To Play and marks In-App Purchases. Those labels describe different parts of the product: free access does not mean that the game contains no paid purchases.

The October release adds G-Coins for buying paid products and states that G-Coins can be purchased from Shop. This reference confirms that entry and purpose. It does not publish a regional price table, bundle catalog, tax calculation, or claim about which purchases are required for progression.

<h2 id="shop">The documented G-Coins entry</h2>

Use the Shop page named by the October 7 announcement when looking for the documented G-Coins purchase entry. Current product contents and prices should be read from the actual purchase interface; no exact pack price or currency conversion was verified in the supplied research.

G-Coins information should also be kept separate from selling eligible loot on the Steam Community Market. [Market basics](/guides/market/) describes that market's stated Steam Wallet funds context. The source does not establish interchangeability between every in-game currency and marketplace funds.

<h2 id="overlay-faq">The official Steam Overlay payment FAQ</h2>

The September 14 FAQ addresses a case where a player has Steam Wallet funds but payment fails. It says that disabling Steam Overlay can affect payment and gives a setting to check in Steam Library: Manage → Properties → Enable the Steam Overlay while in-game.

This is a dated official explanation for that scenario, not a diagnosis of every payment failure. It does not establish the cause of a specific new error, a guaranteed fix, or a reason to repeatedly attempt a purchase. Later client or Steam changes may affect the interface.
