The War of Genesis: Idle Loot market is the Steam Community Market for App 4891320. Official store text describes eligible loot trading, and the market page describes Steam Wallet funds. Trading support does not mean every dropped item is sellable or that listed prices establish reliable earnings.

<h2 id="entry">Open the correct Steam market</h2>

Use the [App 4891320 market search](https://steamcommunity.com/market/search?appid=4891320) to identify listings for this game. The recorded October 10 search results included jewels and Tier 6 cloaks. Those examples show that listings existed for particular items; they are not a complete current catalog.

The official store and market both support the existence of trading. The market describes Steam Wallet funds, so this reference does not relabel proceeds as a guaranteed cash income or project a return from gameplay time.

<h2 id="item-restrictions">Read the restriction on the specific item</h2>

The recorded [Special Jewel of Strife listing](https://steamcommunity.com/market/listings/4891320/Special%20Jewel%20of%20Strife) uses buy and sell orders. Its explanation describes matching the lowest sell order with the highest buy order.

That particular page states that the purchased item cannot be traded and cannot be relisted on the Steam Community Market for one week. These are the restrictions observed for that item on October 10, 2026. Do not apply its exact lock period automatically to every jewel, cloak, or other drop.

<h2 id="delivery">Inventory delivery and older instructions</h2>

The September 17 official update says items purchased or retrieved from Steam Market now go directly into Inventory. This is newer than the September 14 FAQ's Storage List → Claim → Mailbox instructions, so the later announcement is the guidance used here.

See [market buying](/guides/market-buying/) for the documented delivery change and the distinction between a buy order and an actual matched purchase. No account purchase was performed during research, so this page does not promise a delivery time.

<h2 id="limits">Listings are not verified farming returns</h2>

A displayed asking price, active listing count, or market item count does not establish completed sales, a stable drop rate, transaction fees, or hourly farming income. The supplied material does not provide the full inputs for that calculation.

Inspect each item's current market and trade markings for its eligibility. Research last checked October 10, 2026; listing prices and restrictions can change. This page intentionally avoids a profit forecast or developer-revenue estimate.
