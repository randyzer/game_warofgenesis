War of Genesis: Idle Loot vs Path of Idle Old Gods Rising is a comparison of two officially described idle RPGs. Both advertise automatic combat and equipment growth. War of Genesis emphasizes a desktop character and Steam Market trading, while Path of Idle emphasizes a Lovecraftian single-player setting and town building.

<h2 id="positioning">Compare the official product descriptions</h2>

The War of Genesis Steam page describes a free desktop loot RPG with a transparent character on the Windows desktop. Its product description includes Alliance Raid, World Boss, PvP Arena, and eligible loot trading.

Path of Idle: Old Gods Rising describes itself as a Lovecraftian single-player idle RPG. Its store page advertises automatic combat, equipment builds, town building, and five progressively introduced adventure modes. Those are product-description differences, not a measured comparison of pacing or quality.

<h2 id="classes-progression">Classes and progression features</h2>

War of Genesis names Knight, Ranger, and Wizard in its release material. Path of Idle's official description advertises six classes. A different class count does not by itself establish more build variety, better balance, or a longer progression path.

The equipment-building loop is a shared advertised feature. Town building is part of Path of Idle's product description, while desktop presentation and Steam Market integration are prominent in War of Genesis. This comparison stays within those stated features instead of guessing identical mechanics from similar genre labels.

<h2 id="scope">Dates, planned features, and comparison limits</h2>

The store material checked on October 10, 2026 lists War of Genesis's full release as October 7, 2026 and Path of Idle's release as August 13, 2026. War of Genesis had an earlier Early Access start, so its September guides need version context.

Path of Idle's description discusses single-player seasonal challenges being added progressively after launch. That plan is not proof that every seasonal feature was already available at the research date.

No hands-on test of performance, balance, late-game longevity, or purchase value underlies this page. Choose which official description fits the type of play you want to investigate; this page does not declare one game better.
