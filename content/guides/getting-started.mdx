Your first Emberdeep session should establish a repeatable rhythm, not produce a heroic story. The city gives you enough recruits and supplies to learn safely if you resist the urge to solve every visible problem at once. This route takes about twenty minutes and ends with the next expedition ready to launch.

<h2 id="install-the-field-kit">Install the field kit</h2>

Open the Codex from the main menu and choose the standard field layout. Confirm that text remains readable over the mine view, then place the crew strip along an edge that does not cover chamber exits. Enable warnings for low Resolve, damaged gear, and full station queues. Leave advanced probability notes hidden for now; they are useful later but make the first map harder to scan.

At the Gatehouse, inspect your three starting crews without rearranging everyone. Give the first crew one lantern, one treatment wrap, and a simple tool that answers rubble or locked mechanisms. Set the retreat marker to four Resolve. The goal is to learn how information appears, so predictable coverage matters more than maximum damage.

<h2 id="first-descent">First descent</h2>

Choose the Shallow Cistern route and make mapping your purpose. Enter the first unknown chamber, read the hazard forecast, and notice which part is certain versus suspected. After resolving the chamber, pin one adjacent branch and compare its distance with your remaining Resolve. Take it only if the route back stays clear.

Return after two or three chambers even if the crew feels healthy. You should bring back a small amount of ore, at least one map annotation, and a clear sense of which role acted most often. That information is more useful than another blind encounter. If the interface slowed you down, visit [Overlay Controls](/guides/controls/) before the next launch.

<h2 id="before-you-log-off">Before you log off</h2>

Send injuries to the Infirmary and damaged equipment to the Forge. Do not fill either queue: leave one slot open for the next returning crew. At the Archive, start the shortest project connected to your new map. Assemble a second crew with the same four basic responsibilities, even if individual recruits cover them differently.

Finally, pin a single next action: revisit the marked branch, gather repair ore, or test one formation change. Avoid a vague note such as “get stronger.” A concrete handoff lets the next session begin at the Gatehouse instead of inside menus. Once this loop feels natural, the full [How to Play Emberdeep](/guides/how-to-play/) guide explains how three crews sustain it across deeper layers.
