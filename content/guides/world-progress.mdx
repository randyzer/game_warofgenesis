World progress measures what the city can sustain, not simply the deepest chamber one crew has touched. Each layer introduces a new pressure while preserving reasons to revisit earlier routes. Advance when your rotation can absorb that pressure and still fund another expedition.

<h2 id="the-five-layers">The five layers</h2>

The **Cisterns** teach mapping, darkness, and retreat. The **Rootworks** add living barriers that change between visits. The **Bell Foundry** introduces Heat, machinery, and routes that reward precise timing. The **Hollow Ward** tests Resolve with echoes that imitate crew traits. The **Deep Vaults** combine previous pressures and ask for several prepared answers instead of one dominant build.

Opening a gate adds new routes, Archive projects, and Forge patterns. It does not invalidate the layer above. Earlier regions remain useful for common materials, recovery expeditions, and safe tests. Keep at least one mapped supply route in each open layer so a failed deep run does not force another risky descent just to pay for repairs.

<h2 id="difficulty-marks">Difficulty marks</h2>

A route's mark summarizes expected pressure, not a guaranteed outcome. One mark means the hazards fit the layer's basic lessons. Two marks indicate either longer travel or a concentrated obstacle. Three marks combine pressures and assume the crew has a specific answer. A pulsing edge means the Signal Tower expects conditions to change before the route expires.

Read the symbols beside the mark. Darkness, armor, spores, mechanisms, and Resolve pressure tell you why the route is difficult. A three-mark route with familiar armor may be safer for your breaker crew than a two-mark spore route without treatment. Certainty matters too: a pale mark based on old mapping deserves extra supplies or a scouting objective.

<h2 id="when-to-advance">When to advance</h2>

Advance when two crews can complete ordinary two-mark routes in the current layer and return above the Resolve threshold. Your third crew should be healthy enough to cover a short opportunity while the others recover. Stock one full repair cycle, one treatment cycle, and enough common supplies for a retreat that earns nothing.

Before opening the gate, finish station jobs that provide broad value and clear nonessential Forge work. Treat the first trip as reconnaissance. Carry flexible tools, map one safe junction, and return before testing the layer's most dramatic objective. If the new pressure exposes a role gap, repair the rotation through [Crew Building](/guides/squad-building/) instead of forcing the strongest team deeper. A measured first visit makes the new layer part of your world rather than a one-time gamble.
