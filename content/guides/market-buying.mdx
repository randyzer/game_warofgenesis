For War of Genesis: Idle Loot market buying, the September 17 official update says purchased or retrieved Steam Market items are delivered directly to Inventory. It replaces the earlier FAQ's Storage List → Claim → Mailbox route. A recorded jewel listing also explains order matching and a one-week relisting restriction for that specific item.

<h2 id="delivery-route">Use the newer Inventory delivery instruction</h2>

The September 14 FAQ describes claiming through Storage List and then Mailbox. Three days later, the official update changes delivery to Inventory for purchases and retrieved items. This page follows the newer dated statement rather than presenting both paths as simultaneous requirements.

If you are comparing an old tutorial with the announcement, the difference is a documented version change. Research did not include a real purchase or an observed delivery delay, so no number of seconds or minutes is offered as a guaranteed waiting period.

<h2 id="orders">Understand the recorded order-matching example</h2>

The [Special Jewel of Strife listing](https://steamcommunity.com/market/listings/4891320/Special%20Jewel%20of%20Strife) describes buy and sell orders and matching the lowest seller with the highest buyer. An order and a matched purchase are different states; the documented Inventory delivery applies to the purchase or retrieval described by the update.

This explanation is based on that item's market page, not on an executed trade. It does not establish matching speed, a successful price, or an account-specific explanation for an item that a player cannot find.

<h2 id="relisting">Check the restriction before buying for resale</h2>

The recorded jewel listing says the item cannot be traded after purchase and cannot be relisted on the Steam Community Market for one week. The direct-trade restriction and the market-relisting period are separate conditions.

Read the current item's own page rather than assuming every game item shares the same period. The statement here is the October 10, 2026 observation for Special Jewel of Strife; it is not a universal trade-lock table.
