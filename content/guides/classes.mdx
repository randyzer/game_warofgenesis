The War of Genesis: Idle Loot classes named in the October 7 official update are Knight, Ranger, and Wizard. Early Access authors describe different roles for them, but their rankings disagree. Use the class names as confirmed information and the older role descriptions as dated opinion, rather than a current universal tier list.

<h2 id="official-names">The three official class names</h2>

Knight, Ranger, and Wizard appear in the official October release update and in two separately authored September class guides. That agreement supports the names; it does not verify every stat, hero unlock price, or switching condition mentioned elsewhere.

A September roadmap placed a new class in the second quarter of 2027 as a future plan. That is not evidence that a fourth class is already playable. The list on this page is limited to the three names confirmed in the supplied release material.

<h2 id="roles-context">How Early Access authors described their roles</h2>

A September 17 class guide characterizes Knight around HP and defense, Wizard around damage, and Ranger around movement and attack speed. A September 11 author emphasizes Knight survival and lightning area damage, and Wizard elemental output with attention to defense. These are the authors' role summaries from Early Access.

Neither comparison provides matched-level, matched-equipment measurements. The descriptions can explain why different players might investigate different classes, but they should not be read as fixed release-version stat advantages. This page does not assign numerical attack, health, speed, or defense values.

<h2 id="rankings">Why the old rankings do not settle the best class</h2>

The September 17 guide puts Wizard ahead of Knight; the September 11 guide puts Knight ahead of Wizard. Both place Ranger below those two, but agreement on one position is still an author assessment rather than a controlled test of the October version.

The practical consequence is to keep class identity separate from claims about strength. The supplied evidence does not establish a single winner across ordinary stages, Arena, and World Boss. Exact builds, skill sequences, and investment costs remain outside this reference.

<h2 id="names-data">Keep official names separate from data-table labels</h2>

A nonofficial skill index uses Archer and Mage in its filters, whereas the current official material uses Ranger and Wizard. This guide keeps the official names. A table label or build label on a fan resource is not enough to replace the public game terminology or prove that its values match the release client.
